About

I am an M.Phil. student in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Tinghuan Chen. I received my B.Eng. in Measurement and Control Technology and Instrumentation from Wuhan University in 2024.

My research interests span AI acceleration, reconfigurable computing, and electronic design automation. I am particularly interested in the co-design of hardware, compilers, and software, as well as LLM-based automation for hardware design.

Before my graduate studies, I worked at Huawei as a logic engineer and a physical design engineer, with experience in system-level modeling and chip implementation.

Research Interests

  • AI acceleration and domain-specific accelerator architectures
  • Reconfigurable computing and FPGA acceleration
  • Compiler–accelerator and hardware–software co-design
  • LLM-based automation for EDA and hardware design
