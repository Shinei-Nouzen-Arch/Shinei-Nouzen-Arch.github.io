About

Currently, I am an M.Phil. student in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Tinghuan Chen. I received my B.Eng. in Measurement and Control Technology and Instrumentation from Wuhan University in 2024. Previously, I worked at Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. as a Logic Engineer and a Physical Design Engineer.
